# Sources and provenance

Use this index to attribute foundations and find original materials. The package contains original instructions, diagnostic questions, examples, and templates. It does not contain the books or reconstruct their chapters or question collections. Publication metadata, broad coverage, and available previews were checked on **2026-11-23**; complete texts of all books were not obtained or read during creation. Do not imply otherwise when using this skill.

Treat the procedures as a practical synthesis that must be tested in context. Author credentials, publisher descriptions, university adoption, and successful case studies support relevance; they do not establish that using a book or this skill causes commercial success.

## Attribution and research practice

Attribute a book's own framework to its author when discussing it. Attribute original proposals to the current design work; do not invent a page reference, quotation, endorsement, or named method. Link the relevant source when an external fact matters. Use ordinary Markdown citations in exported documents and the environment's citation format in chat when available.

For a deeper book-specific task, consult a lawfully accessible copy or user-provided passages. Use authorized previews and summaries when full access is unavailable, and state the resulting limits. Keep copyrighted manuscripts, extensive extracts, publisher graphics, and credentialed content out of repositories.

Refresh unstable facts before deciding: platform policies and visibility, engine capabilities, storefront requirements, current comparable-game performance, pricing, and release/festival dates. Use official documentation for platform rules and firsthand accounts for historical decisions. Identify reported numbers, estimates, and your own inferences separately.
